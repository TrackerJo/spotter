## Known limits

- Nearby networking is encrypted direct Wi-Fi / LAN, not a Bluetooth-only transport. Wi-Fi must stay enabled. Internet delivery checks every five seconds in the foreground; background delivery is not guaranteed.
- Joined friends can log sets for each other automatically. Shared sessions are intended for two trusted friends.
- Local solo history has no CloudKit backup or new-install recovery in this version. Only explicitly shared sessions synchronize.
- Local storage rewrites an atomic JSON snapshot; the append-only audit history has no compaction yet.
- Shared participants finish independently. Ending your session removes only you from the live session while preserving your history; the shared workout becomes complete after all participants finish. New sets are recorded only on Log set; legacy drafts stay preserved and excluded from displayed sets and statistics.
