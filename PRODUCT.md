# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Two friends logging training between sets, often holding a phone in one hand in a brightly lit gym with unreliable internet. They train together or independently.

## Product Purpose
Fast, trustworthy personal workout records and transparent progression. An empty workout becomes a record only through deliberate exercise and set choices.

## Brand Personality
Practical, calm, precise. Native iPhone conventions; no social feed or lifestyle dashboard.

## Design Principles
Keep the person being logged unmistakable. Put previous performance beside current effort. Recommendations never become recorded results. Preserve personal history and safe concurrent edits. Nearby connection must work without gym internet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
System typography with Dynamic Type, system light/dark appearance, meaningful VoiceOver labels, minimum 44-point controls, and textual status alongside color.
